Hero Project

Privacy-Preserving LLM Pipeline & Token Provenance

Feeding confidential legal contracts, medical reports, or corporate documents into commercial cloud LLMs exposes sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (PII) to training pipelines and potential data breaches. SafePrompt solves this by acting as an intelligent privacy shield directly on the user's machine:

  • Local NLP Entity Recognition: Inspects documents (`.docx`) locally using spaCy (pt_core_news) and regex patterns to identify names, CPFs, email addresses, phone numbers, and physical addresses.
  • Reversible Data Sanitization: Substitutes sensitive entities with contextually coherent synthetic placeholders or synthetic tokens before the prompt leaves the device.
  • Auditable Mapping Engine: Generates an immutable .audit.json file and an encrypted .mapping.json table, enabling full bidirectional document reconstruction when the AI model returns its completion.
  • Token Provenance & Transparency Panel: Validates each token's origin, ensuring evaluators can verify whether text was user-authored, locally sanitized, or generated by the LLM.
