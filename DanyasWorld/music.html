Music

“I have always loved music since I was a child. I have loved the piano since I found an old keyboard at my grandma's farm and played a random, rusty note for the first time. The main piece is called ‘Underwater Wonderescapes’ and it's my dad's favorite. On the flute, it's my mom's favorite song. On the guitar, I chose my sister's signature tune, and on performance three — a duet with my friend who did not play the piano until an hour before we recorded that. I think these videos say more about me than the fancy piano pieces, like the first one, that I can play. They make me feel human.”
